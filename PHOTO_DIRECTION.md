# Photography direction

The website should use bright, realistic corporate software photography rather than dark command-centre imagery.

## Preferred visual style

- Modern daylight offices
- Software engineers collaborating naturally
- Real monitors, laptops, whiteboards and product discussions
- Neutral clothing and believable work environments
- Balanced diversity without staged corporate poses
- Light grey, white, soft blue and natural wood surroundings
- Subtle blue/cyan colour treatment only where it supports the brand

## Avoid

- Night-time control rooms
- Surveillance-heavy imagery as the company-wide identity
- Cyberpunk neon
- Holograms and floating interfaces
- Generic handshakes
- Fake client logos
- Overly staged stock-photo smiles

## Current homepage photography

The homepage uses free-to-use Pexels photography for the hero and engineering-process section. These URLs should eventually be replaced with locally hosted, licensed originals or a commissioned Third Leap Labs photo shoot.
