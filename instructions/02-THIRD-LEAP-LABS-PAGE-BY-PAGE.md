# Third Leap Labs Website — Page-by-Page Content and Layout

## Site objective

Position Third Leap Labs as an applied AI product company with credible engineering depth. Cognisense Cloud is the flagship product. WhaleScanner and open-source work demonstrate broader execution capability without distracting from the AI and computer-vision narrative.

## Global header

### Logo

Third Leap Labs

### Navigation

- Home
- Cognisense Cloud
- Products
- Capabilities
- Open Source
- Company
- Contact

### Primary CTA

**Explore Cognisense** → `https://cognisense.cam`

### Header behaviour

- Transparent over the hero, becoming a subtle solid or blurred background on scroll.
- Compact height and generous horizontal spacing.
- Keep the primary CTA visible on desktop.
- On mobile, use a clean full-screen or sheet-style navigation.

## Section 6 — Engineering principles

### Headline

# Built with discipline.

### Principles

**Human-centred**  
AI should help people understand and act, not hide decisions behind a black box.

**Deployment-aware**  
Real systems must account for privacy, latency, connectivity, hardware and operational constraints.

**Interoperable**  
Products should connect with existing infrastructure instead of forcing unnecessary replacement.

**Evidence-led**  
Every important claim, alert and workflow should be traceable to useful context.

**Secure by design**  
Access, data handling and operational control must be part of the architecture from the beginning.

# Mobile rules

- Preserve large typography but prevent awkward one-word wrapping.
- Convert product interface compositions into swipeable or vertically stacked views.
- Avoid tiny multi-column feature cards.
- Keep CTA buttons full-width where appropriate.
- Ensure product visuals remain legible rather than merely shrinking desktop layouts.
