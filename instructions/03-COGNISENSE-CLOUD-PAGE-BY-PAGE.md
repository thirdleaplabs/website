## FAQ

### Does Cognisense replace our cameras?

Cognisense is designed to work with existing compatible camera infrastructure and approved video sources. Compatibility is confirmed during discovery.

### Is Cognisense only a cloud CCTV system?

No. Cognisense is a visual-intelligence and incident-workflow platform that connects cameras, edge processing, events, evidence and analytics.

### Can video remain on site?

Cognisense can support deployment patterns in which continuous video remains local and selected events, metadata or evidence are sent centrally. The exact architecture depends on scope.

### Does AI make decisions automatically?

Cognisense can automate event detection and workflow steps, but important incidents are designed to remain reviewable by authorised users.

### How does a pilot begin?

A pilot begins with a site, a compatible camera set, a defined use case and agreed success criteria.
